Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Wylo and tap Cancel Subscription, or in Wylo go to your dog's tab → Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before, then go to Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription.

How do I delete my account?

In Wylo go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, your dog's profile, plans, chats and photos, and revokes Sign in with Apple. It can't be undone. Cancel your subscription separately in the App Store.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In Wylo go to Settings → Export my data. You'll get a JSON file with your profile, your dog, plans, memory notes and conversations, plus links to your photos (valid for 7 days). Save it or share it anywhere you like.

Can I turn off reminders or analytics?

Yes. Reminders are under Settings → Reminders, and product analytics can be switched off under Settings → Privacy. We never send your messages, photos, name, email or your dog's name to analytics.

Is Wylo veterinary advice?

No. Wylo gives training guidance, not veterinary advice. For illness, injury, sudden behaviour changes or any bite, contact a vet or a certified behaviour professional.
