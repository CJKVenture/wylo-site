Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

  1. Information we collect. Account (a user ID from Sign in with Apple or Google, your email address, and your first name if the sign-in provider shares it); dog profile (name, breed, age, sex, neuter status, weight, source, household, photos); coaching content (messages, photos, logs, memory notes); usage/device data; subscription status (billing via Apple — we don't receive full card details). No precise location data is collected. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  2. How we use it. Provide and personalize coaching (including generating AI responses), maintain the dog's profile/memory, operate features, process subscriptions, analyze and improve, ensure safety and prevent abuse, communicate, comply with law.
  3. AI processing & third parties. To generate responses we send relevant content to Anthropic (Claude API). Service providers: Anthropic (AI), Supabase (database/auth/ storage), Google and Apple (sign-in), RevenueCat and Apple (subscriptions), and PostHog (product analytics; we send an anonymous user ID and in-app events, never your messages, photos, name, email, or your dog's name). Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, content we send is not used to train its models. We do NOT sell or "share" your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. You can turn analytics off in Settings. AI features require your permission: the App asks before anything is sent to Anthropic, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings (Privacy → AI coaching).
  4. Legal bases (GDPR/UK). Contract performance, consent, legitimate interests, legal obligations.
  5. Retention. Kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data and photos are deleted from our active systems right away. Any remaining copies in service-provider backups or logs expire within 30 days, except where law requires longer retention.
  6. Security. Encryption in transit, access controls, row-level data isolation. No method is 100% secure; breach notification as required by law.
  7. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA). Access, correct, delete, export; object/restrict; opt out of "sale"/"sharing" (we don't sell); no discrimination for exercising rights. In-app controls in Settings; requests to cjkventure@gmail.com.
  8. Data deletion & export. In Settings you can export a copy of your data and permanently delete your account. Deleting the App alone doesn't delete server data or cancel the subscription.
  9. Children's privacy. For users 18+; not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
  10. International transfers. Processed in the US and elsewhere; appropriate safeguards where required.
  11. Cookies / SDKs. The App uses SDKs for sign-in, subscriptions, and analytics as described above. We use no advertising SDKs and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  12. Apple App Privacy. Our App Store privacy label reflects this Policy; this Policy governs if they differ.
  13. Changes. Material changes notified in-app with an updated effective date.
  14. Contact. cjkventure@gmail.com, CJK Venture LLC.
